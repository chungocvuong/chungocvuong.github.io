About

I'm an AI engineer with 2 years building production LLM systems — retrieval-augmented search, multi-agent pipelines, and the serving infrastructure around them.

I care about the boring parts that make systems trustworthy: zero-downtime reindexing, fail-open degradation under load, evaluation harnesses that separate real regressions from model sampling noise. Published at VLSP 2025 (ACL), placed first at ImageCLEFmed MEDIQA-CORE 2026.

Outside work I research AI for health — dementia and Alzheimer's disease, over ADNI cohorts — with the Data Science and Knowledge Technology Laboratory at UET. This is where I'll write about what I build and what I learn debugging it.
